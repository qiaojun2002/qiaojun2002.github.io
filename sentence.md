## Day 1
```
What would be your perfect day?
你完美的一天是怎么样的？ 
```

What would be什么会是，什么可能是，比what is 意思更地道

what would be 可以替换为：
- who would be
- where would be
		
perfect	可以替换为：
- 	ideal
- 	preferred
- 	worst
	
***
			
			
```
I mean your ideal day
我的意思是你的理想的一天
```

解释作用

***

```
A day that you wnet to be that night and you just thought, 
that was the most incredeble day!!
```

the most incredeble day可以替换为：
- the most incredible day
- the most memorable day
- the most boring day


			
## Day 2
```
That is how I want most of my days and my list to be just like that!!
我希望我大部分的日子和生命都能那样度过
```


> 句型1： That is how I want + 名词 + to + 动词			

that is how I want my boyfriend to look like.

that is how i want my live to be lived.

> 句型2： That is how I want + to + 动词	

that is how i want to live my live.

want 可以替换为：
- feel
- think

***

```
what would that perfect day be like for you
这样完美的一天对你来说是什么样的
```

对比第一天的例句
what would be your perfect day?
它们句型不一样，它们意思是一样的
同样的意思，用不同的句型去表达
