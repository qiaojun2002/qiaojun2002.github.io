- where would be


perfect	可以替换为：

- preferred
- worst

## Day 2
```
That is how I want most of my days and my life to be just like that!!
我希望我大部分的日子和生命都能那样度过
```


> 句型1： That is how I want + 名词 + to + 动词			

that is how I want my boyfriend to look like.

that is how i want my live to be lived.

> 句型2： That is how I want + to + 动词	

that is how i want to live my live.

want 可以替换为：
- feel
- think

***

```
What would that perfect day be like for you?
这样完美的一天对你来说是什么样的
```

对比第一天的例句
what would be your perfect day?
它们句型不一样，它们意思是一样的
同样的意思，用不同的句型去表达

## Day 3

```
I am the one that controls whether or not I am gonna have a good day
我是那种无论怎么样都能掌控并好好度过每一天的人
```
control  
in control of<br>
I am the one in control of whether or not I am gonna have a good day

把control 和 in control of 放到一个句子中<br>
示例： 
Even though we can not control what happens around us, we are in control of our reactions and emotions.

whether or not，是否<br>
示例：
We are debating whether or not we should join Steve's oral English activity.

whether or not + to + 动词<br>
We are debating whether or not to join Steve's oral English activity.<br>
She's thinking whether of not to quit her job.
