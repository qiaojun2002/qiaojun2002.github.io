```
What would be your perfect day?
你完美的一天是怎么样的？ 
```

What would be什么会是，什么可能是，比what is 意思更地道

what would be 可以替换为：
- who would be
- where would be


perfect	可以替换为：
- ideal
- preferred
- worst

## Day 2
```
That is how I want most of my days and my life to be just like that!!
我希望我大部分的日子和生命都能那样度过
```


> 句型1： That is how I want + 名词 + to + 动词			

that is how I want my boyfriend to look like.

that is how i want my live to be lived.

> 句型2： That is how I want + to + 动词	

that is how i want to live my live.

want 可以替换为：
- feel
- think

***

```
What would that perfect day be like for you?
这样完美的一天对你来说是什么样的
```

对比第一天的例句
what would be your perfect day?
它们句型不一样，它们意思是一样的
同样的意思，用不同的句型去表达

## Day 3

```
I am the one that controls whether or not I am gonna have a good day
我是那种无论怎么样都能掌控并好好度过每一天的人
```
control  
in control of<br>
I am the one in control of whether or not I am gonna have a good day

把control 和 in control of 放到一个句子中<br>
示例： 
Even though we can not control what happens around us, we are in control of our reactions and emotions.

whether or not，是否<br>
示例：
We are debating whether or not we should join Steve's oral English activity.

whether or not + to + 动词<br>
We are debating whether or not to join Steve's oral English activity.<br>
She's thinking whether of not to quit her job.

```
All of us, we are the ones that define if negative impacts are gonna have a good or bad impact on your life.
我们所有人，负面的东西对我们产生怎么样的影响，取决于我们自己。
```

All of us, 对比于everyone，更加强调个人责任。<br>
示例1：<br>
It's up to all of us to save the world.
需要我们`每个人`来拯救世界。

示例2：<br>
All of us must be conscious of our lifestyle to protect our environment.
`我们都`需要对我们的生活方式有所察觉才能保护我们的环境。

gonna 是going to 的缩写

wanna = want to
gotta = got to

## Day 4

```
It's your mentality, your mind is a battleground.
这是你的心态，你的思想是战场
```

mentality 强调脑子里的想法，近义词：
- perspective, 看问题的角度
- attitude，看待事情的态度

It's your negative mantality that's blocking you from achieving your maximum potential / becoming your best side.
是你的负面心态阻止了你成为更好的自己。

虽然它们是近义词，但具体语境下不能互换。
Whether our attitude is positive or negative depends on our perspective of seeing things.
我们的态度是积极的还是消极的取决于我们看待事情的角度。

We have to constantly fight the negative voices in our heads.
我们要不断的与脑中消极的声音作斗争。
